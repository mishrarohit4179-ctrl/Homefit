📚 Formula Kya Use Hoti Hai?

Yeh calculator Mifflin-St Jeor equation use karta hai — yeh sabse accurate BMR formula mana jaata hai modern nutrition science mein.

Pehle BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate) nikala jaata hai — matlab aaram karte waqt body ko kitni calories chahiye. Phir usse activity multiplier se multiply karke TDEE nikala jaata hai.

Men: BMR = (10 × weight kg) + (6.25 × height cm) − (5 × age) + 5 Women: BMR = (10 × weight kg) + (6.25 × height cm) − (5 × age) − 161 TDEE = BMR × Activity Multiplier
