💡 Health Tips
ℹ️ BMI ke baare mein
BMI (Body Mass Index) ek simple calculation hai jo weight aur height se body fat estimate karta hai. Yeh sirf ek indicator hai — actual health ke liye doctor se consult karo. Athletes, pregnant women aur bacchon ke liye results different ho sakte hain.
