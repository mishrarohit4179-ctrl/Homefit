🤔 HIIT Kya Hota Hai?

High Intensity Interval Training ek workout method hai jismein short bursts of intense exercise aur recovery periods alternate hote hain. Jaise: 30 second sprint, phir 15 second rest — yeh cycle repeat hoti hai.

Regular cardio se zyada effective hai — kam time mein zyada calories burn hoti hain aur workout ke baad bhi body fat burn karta rehta hai (Afterburn / EPOC effect).
