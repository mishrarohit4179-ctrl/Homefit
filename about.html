📖 Our Story
Shuru Kaise Hua?

HomeFit ki kahani shuru hoti hai ek simple problem se — gym expensive hai, time nahi hota, aur ghar pe workout karna boring lagta hai. Hum chahte the ek aisi jagah banani jahan koi bhi, bina kisi equipment ke, apni fitness journey shuru kar sake.

Bahut saare log fitness apps download karte hain jo ya toh paid hote hain, ya itne complicated ki shuru karne ka mann hi nahi karta. HomeFit ka idea yahi tha — ekdum simple, free aur motivating platform jo beginners se lekar intermediate tak sabke kaam aaye.

Aaj HomeFit mein 30-Day Challenges, BMI Calculator, Calorie Tracker, HIIT Timer aur Progress Tracker hain — sab kuch completely free, koi ads nahi, koi account nahi. Sirf tum aur tumhari fitness.

🎯 Our Mission
Hamaari Soch

Hamara mission simple hai — fitness ko accessible banana. India mein aaj bhi bahut saare log gym afford nahi kar paate, ya unke sheher mein achha gym nahi hai, ya phir unke paas itna waqt nahi ki regularly gym jaayein.

Hum believe karte hain ki fitness sirf paison walon ka privilege nahi hona chahiye. Ek smartphone aur thodi si jagah ho toh tum apni poori body transform kar sakte ho — HomeFit yahi prove karna chahta hai.

Hamara goal hai ek aisa platform banana jo scientifically backed ho, practically useful ho, aur itna simple ho ki koi bhi use kar sake — chahe student ho, office worker ho, ya ghar par rehne wala koi bhi.
